# Blender Cheat Sheet & Quick Reference
Source: https://quickref.me/blender.html
Retrieved: 2026-07-22
Note: This reference contains a mix of legacy (pre-2.8) and modern shortcuts. 
Modern Blender (2.8+/4.x) replaced many F-key context switches with workspaces and pie menus.
F-key mappings (F4-F10) are from the legacy 2.4x era and NOT applicable to 4.x.
